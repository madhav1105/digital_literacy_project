For creating the infographic, I used Canva, a free and beginner-friendly design platform that provides ready-made templates, icons, and font options to help create visually appealing content without any prior design knowledge.
The infographic I designed focuses on raising digital literacy awareness among students. It covers several important topics — starting with what digital literacy actually means in today's world. It then moves into internet safety tips like choosing strong passwords and being cautious of unknown or suspicious links. I also included a section on building a professional digital presence using platforms such as LinkedIn and GitHub, which are increasingly important for students entering the job market.
The design further addresses the basics of email communication, emphasizing the need for a proper subject line, polite greeting, and professional closing in every email. Cybercrime awareness was another key area I highlighted, particularly the importance of never sharing your OTP or UPI PIN with anyone, regardless of the situation. Finally, I added a section on helpful digital tools like Canva, Google Forms, GitHub, and HackerRank that students can use for learning and collaboration.
What I found most interesting during this task was discovering how much visual design choices — like color contrast, font size, and layout spacing — affect how information is received. The biggest challenge was fitting all the content into a single page without making it look cluttered. This task was a great introduction to both digital awareness and the basics of visual communication.
